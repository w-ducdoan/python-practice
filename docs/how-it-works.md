# How this site works

[Back to Home](index.md)

## A session

1. **Review (about 8 minutes).** The Home page lists exercises that are due. Each opens with a fresh editor, and you solve it again from memory.
2. **Next lesson (about 30 minutes).** Read "The idea," play with the "Try it" example, then work through the exercises in order.
3. **Explain it back (2 minutes).** Answer the question at the end in your own words, then open the model answer and compare.

## Exercises

- **Run** executes your code and shows what it printed. Use it as often as you like.
- **Check** runs hidden tests and tells you what passed and what didn't. Your result is saved.
- **Hint** shows one hint at a time. Try for a few minutes before you open one.
- **Show solution** unlocks after your first Check. Read it after you pass, too, since it may be cleaner than yours.
- **Ctrl+Enter** is a shortcut for Run, and **Ctrl+Shift+Enter** for Check. **Tab** inserts four spaces.

Python runs inside your browser, so there's nothing to install. The first Run on a page takes about 10 seconds while Python loads.

## Review schedule

When you pass an exercise, it comes back for review after 2 days, then 7, 21, 60 and 120. Pass a review and the gap grows. Miss one and the exercise is marked "missed," and it starts the schedule over once you pass it again.

## Checkpoints

Each module ends with a checkpoint: no hints, and the solution stays locked until you pass. A module is **mastered** when every exercise is passed, the checkpoint is passed, and no reviews are missed. Move on only once the module is mastered.

## Your progress

Progress is saved in this browser only. Use **Export progress** on the Home page every few weeks so you have a backup, and use **Import progress** on another computer.
